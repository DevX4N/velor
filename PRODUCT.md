# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Next.js (App Router) + React + TypeScript + Tailwind CSS, GSAP + ScrollTrigger for scroll choreography, React Three Fiber + Drei + Three.js for the bottle. Pinned by the user's brief. Local only for now (port 3020); publishing to GitHub/Vercel is deferred until the user asks.

## Users

Primary audience is people evaluating the author's Creative Development portfolio (studios, art directors, clients hiring for premium brand experiences). Inside the fiction, the visitor is someone discovering a rare luxury fragrance.

## Product Purpose

A cinematic, scroll-driven brand experience for VÉLOR Nº 01, a fictional French-style luxury extrait de parfum. Success: the visitor feels they watched a fragrance campaign film, and an evaluator sees command of WebGL, motion and art direction.

## Positioning

VÉLOR — The Art of Presence. Mystery, elegance, intensity, exclusivity. "A presença não precisa de palavras."

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Fictional brand; no real commerce. The CTA opens an editorial panel with fragrance details, never a fake checkout.
- The bottle is procedural 3D (cap, sprayer, body as independent groups) and must be swappable for a GLB later without rewriting animations.
- Seven scenes: Awakening (preloader + hero), Revelation, Anatomy of Luxury (real 3D exploded view), Essence (three notes: Bergamot / Black Amber / Vetiver & Oud), Art of Presence (kinetic type), Object of Desire (ivory set + CTA), Final Statement (monumental wordmark + footer).
- Language: mixed, as in the brief — campaign titles in English/French, signature lines and descriptive copy in Portuguese.
- Must respect prefers-reduced-motion, work on mobile without heavy pinning, no scroll-jacking.

## Brand Commitments

- Name: VÉLOR — Parfums. Product: VÉLOR Nº 01 — Extrait de Parfum, 50 ML.
- Palette pinned by the user: #080808, #141414, #C4A77D (champagne gold), #F0E9DF (ivory), #292929.
- Typography pinned in kind: high-contrast editorial serif for titles, light refined sans for secondary text.
- Quiet luxury. Banned by the user: SaaS components, shadowed cards, loud gradients, template sections, decorative icon clutter, purposeless flashy animation, futuristic look.

## Evidence on Hand

None. No photography, no 3D model, no real product data. Notes and materials (bergamot, black amber, vetiver & oud; smoked glass, brushed gold cap, 50 ml) come from the brief. Do not invent prices, stockists, awards or reviews.

## Product Principles

1. The bottle is the protagonist; every scene serves it.
2. One continuous film, not stacked sections.
3. Elegance over quantity: slow, physical, precise motion.
4. Nothing promises an action that does not exist.
